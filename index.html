Commission notes

Prices have moved to VGen and Toyhouse. Price range depends on character complexity; custom prices also depend on complexity.

Every commission is paid work. Add-ons (OC, background, object) are charged. Clutter and decoration count toward character design complexity. Every custom gets free art.

Commercial use is charged. 🫶 Off-site custom payments are due upfront or after the sketch. Only PayPal is connected on VGen.

Turnaround time is listed in commission details. Updates are sent every phase; Pawio will let clients know if there are delays.

Payment methods listed on Pawio's card: PayPal, VGen, bank transfer, and local payment apps. Other methods like Nitro, decor, and game top-ups are accepted only when announced.
